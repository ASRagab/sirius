## Toasts

Keep toasts to one plain sentence, two only when the news needs it, under 140 characters. A toast shows four lines of about 55 characters and nothing past them, so never send more than fits. Plain text; the only formatting shown is `- ` bullets, **bold** and `code`. Use a list rarely: a short lead line and at most three items, each under 50 characters so it stays on one line.
