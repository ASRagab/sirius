# Product intent

Last updated: `2026.10.27`.

> Sirius is your daimon: an intimate extension of your working memory, and a very fast router for every intention that passes through it.

## Objectives

1. **It's always available.** You can always speak to the main thread, even while work is running. A message is acknowledged at once, never queued behind something else.
2. **It's always a router.** The main thread routes and responds; it doesn't do the deep work. Anything that takes more than a moment goes to an agent, so the thread stays free for your next thought.
3. **It follows what you're attending to.** It knows what you're looking at and what you just said, so "this" and "that one" need no explaining.
4. **It speaks once.** Each piece of news reaches you once, briefly, at the right moment. Anything that needs more context goes to a durable surface instead: a note or document it writes and opens for you in Paper. Only things that need you (a decision, a block, a result) interrupt.
5. **It works with what you already use.** Your apps, your agents and your other sessions, without asking them to change. A Claude Code or Codex install is the only requirement.
